MATCH CONTEXT

What counted as pressure?

These rules are applied before the delivery. Counts use a priority tag so overlapping pressure conditions are counted once: first-innings stress, high required rate, then chase with wickets / late pressure.

01
◷

First-innings stress

At least 4 wickets down with 30 or fewer legal balls left; or current run rate below 8 with 18 or fewer balls left; or at least 2 wickets down with 12 or fewer balls left.

WICKETS / LOW RR IN FINAL OVERS— deliveries tagged
02
↗

High required rate

In a chase, required rate is at least 9.0 with 60 or fewer legal balls remaining, and the target has not been reached.

RRR ≥ 9.0 · ≤60 BALLS LEFT— deliveries tagged
03
▥

Chase with wickets / late pressure

Required rate is at least 8.0 with 5 or more wickets down and 90 or fewer legal balls left; or at least 7.5 with 30 or fewer balls left. The target must not be reached.

RRR ≥8 WITH WICKETS / ≥7.5 LATE— deliveries tagged

05 / MODEL DESIGN

How the rankings are built

Every component is scaled to a percentile across eligible players, then combined with the published weights below.

A

Batter score

100%
Pressure strike rate (30%), strike-rate uplift (25%), smoothed-average uplift (15%), boundary-rate uplift (15%) and pressure-innings consistency (15%).
B

Bowler score

100%
Wickets per 24 legal balls (28%), pressure economy (24%), economy improvement (18%), dot-ball rate (12%), dot-rate uplift (8%) and pressure-outing consistency (10%).
BATTER ELIGIBILITY150+ pressure balls8+ pressure matches · 3+ seasons · 100+ baseline balls · 50%+ balls at positions 1–7
BOWLER ELIGIBILITY150+ legal pressure balls8+ pressure matches · 3+ seasons · 100+ baseline legal balls

READ BEFORE USING THE RANKINGS
  • The supplied formal brief specifies IPL and T20I data. Two other T20 competitions in the metadata are excluded from this primary ranking.
  • IPL and T20I are combined for the main ranking; opposition strength and match environments are not identical.
  • Pressure thresholds and scoring weights are transparent analyst choices, not official RR ratings.
  • The organiser pack has no nationality column. At the participant’s request, Pakistan- and Bangladesh-affiliated T20I player IDs are excluded using team mapping as a proxy; this cannot verify every player’s nationality.
  • Retired/inactive status, current fitness, auction registration, availability and franchise eligibility are not inferred. This is a retrospective performance ranking, not an auction eligibility list.
  • The output is a relative ranking among eligible players in this dataset, not a guarantee of future IPL performance.
